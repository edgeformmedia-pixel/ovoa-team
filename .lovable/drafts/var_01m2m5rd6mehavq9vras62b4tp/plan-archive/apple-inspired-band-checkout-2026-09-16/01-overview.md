# Apple-inspired Band checkout

## What will change
- Replace the soft neumorphic checkout with a crisp, bright product-buying layout inspired by the supplied reference.
- Add a slim product and price bar, a large pale-gray Band gallery on the left, and a compact purchase panel on the right.
- Keep the existing four Band views, `$99` price, contact, shipping, payment fields, and visual-only order confirmation.
- Make the layout stack cleanly on phones while keeping the desktop product view dominant.
- Preserve Ovoa/Band identity and imagery; no Apple names, logos, copy, or watch controls will be copied.

## Visual direction
White canvas, subtle gray dividers, near-black text, restrained blue focus/action color, compact rounded selectors, and generous product-focused whitespace. Inter SemiBold remains the site-wide type.

## Scope
Only `/checkout` and its checkout-specific presentation will change. The landing page and Band app remain untouched.
