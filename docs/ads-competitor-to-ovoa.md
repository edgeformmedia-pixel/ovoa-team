## Rules carried over (from the case study)

- **Real reviews only**, first name + initial + city, with permission. Ask the first paying members by text.
- **No fake "was" prices or permanent sales** (FTC risk); the free trial is the offer.
- **Ship in waves**: 10–15 creatives, keep what survives 2–3 weeks, duplicate winners into new ad sets,
  relaunch instead of retiring. `ovoa-ads-weekly-review` can make the Scale/Keep/Kill calls.
- **Start with #1 + #2 + #5** (outcome shot, one promise, "the whole interface is a text").
