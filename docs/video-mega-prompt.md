# OVOA ad: video creator mega prompt

Paste the block below into the admin.ovoa.ai Studio prompt box.

```
Create a 15-second vertical 9:16 social video ad, photorealistic, shot like a premium iPhone commercial: natural daylight, shallow depth of field, handheld but steady camera, warm, clean, modern color grade, lots of breathing room in the frame. No logos other than the ones described. No subtitles burned in unless described.

CHARACTER: a relaxed, friendly woman in her early 30s, casual-smart outfit (light knit sweater, small tote bag), natural makeup. She is busy but calm. She holds a black iPhone with the screen facing the camera when shown.

SCENE 1 (0–3s) — HOOK: Close-up as she steps out of a glass office door onto a sunny city sidewalk, mid-stride, looking at her phone. Quick, confident motion. The feeling: "I just remembered something I can't forget."

SCENE 2 (3–7s) — THE TEXT: Over-the-shoulder shot of her iPhone in the Apple Messages app, a plain iMessage conversation with a contact named "OVOA" (round black avatar with a white "O"). She types and sends a blue bubble: "remind me to call the vet friday". Within a second a gray reply bubble appears: "Done ✅ Friday 10am. I'll text you." The phone UI is crisp, clean, realistic, iOS style, white background.

SCENE 3 (7–11s) — THE PAYOFF: Cut to a few days later, same woman at home in a bright kitchen, coffee in hand. Her phone on the counter lights up with an iMessage notification from "OVOA": "Reminder: call the vet 🐶". She smiles, picks it up. Then a quick wide shot of her laughing on a call while a happy golden retriever sits next to her.

SCENE 4 (11–15s) — END CARD: Clean off-white background with soft gray gradient toward the bottom. Large, bold, black, centered sans-serif text, two lines: "Just text it." / "OVOA remembers." Below it, smaller: "Your first 15 texts are free · ovoa.ai/text". A simple black rounded button that reads "Text OVOA" gently pulses once.

STYLE NOTES: minimal, Apple-like, human, optimistic. No futuristic holograms, no robots, no glowing AI brains, no sci-fi UI. The AI is invisible; the only "product" is a normal text thread. Smooth match cuts between scenes. Soft upbeat acoustic/lo-fi music bed, a single iMessage "swoosh" sound when the text sends and the familiar ding when the reply and the reminder arrive.

AVOID: extra fingers, distorted hands, garbled or misspelled on-screen text, fake app icons, cluttered backgrounds, other brand names, dark or moody lighting.
```

## Variants (swap Scenes 2–3 and keep everything else)

- **Website:** she texts "make me a website for my bakery" → OVOA: "Here it is 🎉 maya.ovoa.ai". Payoff: a customer scrolling the bakery site on a phone at the counter. End card: "Your website, built by a text."
- **Group chat:** friends at a dinner table; one texts the group "OVOA split this 4 ways" → OVOA: "$23.50 each. I'll remind whoever hasn't paid." End card: "Split it. Poll it. Plan it."
- **Trip:** "plan a weekend in Miami under $600" → OVOA replies with a 3-line plan. Payoff: beach. End card: "Your trip, planned in a text."
- **Student:** "my essay is due thursday" → "I'll nudge you Tues and Wed night 📚". End card: "Due dates that text you first."
- **6-second hook cut:** use Scene 2 only, then the end card.

## Tips

- Video models often garble on-screen text. If the bubbles or end card come out wrong, generate without text
  ("blank chat bubbles", "blank end card") and add the words in an editor or CapCut.
- If the model caps at 5–10s, generate each scene as its own clip with the same CHARACTER and STYLE lines, then stitch them together.
- Don't put real customer names or reviews in generated video.
