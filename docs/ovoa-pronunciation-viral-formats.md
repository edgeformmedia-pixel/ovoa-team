## Format 1: "Say the word on beat" (the trend itself)
**Hook text:** "Say the word on beat" trend, but the word is my phone's assistant.
**Setup:** TV rhythm game, 5 rounds. Rounds 1-4 are easy rhymes (boat, coat, goat, float). Round 5 is the OVOA logo.
**Beat:** He says "OH-vo-a", "oh-VOA", "O-V-O-A" over the beat. The screen then shows **oh-VOH-uh** and a voice says it. Round it out with him getting it right on the second try.
**Caption:** "5/5 and I still can't say it 😭 it's oh-VOH-uh #saythewordonbeat"
**Pronunciation emphasis:** the correct way is the last frame, voiced and subtitled with the stressed syllable in caps.

## Format 2: "Words my boyfriend can't pronounce"
**Hook text:** "Giving my boyfriend words I don't think he can pronounce."
**Setup:** A list on paper or phone: quinoa, espresso, gyro, **OVOA**.
**Beat:** He nails the first three. OVOA is the last, he reads "O-vo-A", she corrects him with the exact stress, he repeats, she grades it.
**Caption:** "It's oh-VOH-uh. The app that texts you first."
**Pronunciation emphasis:** the correction is the middle of the video; the repeat-it-back is the ending.

**Beat:** B texts "remind me to call mom" and OVOA's reply arrives. B says "it's oh-VOH-uh, it texts YOU first." A repeats it right.

## Format 5: "Duet this: say OVOA" (UGC chain)
**Hook text:** "Say OVOA. Wrong answers only."
**Setup:** A stitchable template. Creator says it wrong on camera, a teammate or text-to-speech says it right in the Stitch.
**Beat:** Stitch chain: 3 wrong versions, 1 correct. Each stitcher adds their own wrong pronunciation.
**Caption:** "Stitch with how you've been saying it. Correct way: oh-VOH-uh."
**Pronunciation emphasis:** the correct version is the pinned comment and the reply sound. Make a **sound named "How to say OVOA"** that anyone can reuse on TikTok.

## Rules for all 5

1. Hook text in the first 3 s, in the trend's own words ("Say the word on beat").
2. Say it correctly at least twice, ideally with a rhyme or a beat drop on the stressed syllable.
3. Put the phonetics on screen: **oh-VOH-uh**, with the stressed syllable bolded or capitalized.
4. Never use an AI voice that gets it wrong. Test every voice-over line before publishing.
5. End card: "OVOA, it texts you first. ovoa.ai/text".
6. Reuse the sound across all five so TikTok groups them.
